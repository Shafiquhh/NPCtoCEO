# NPCtoCEO 

A gamified daily life simulator and clarity tracker inspired by Tamagotchi and flowchart builders. Log your daily habits, map your day on an interactive canvas, and let your cynical Gen-Z avatar brutally roast or praise your life choices in real-time.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive Canvas Flowchart:** Powered by **Drawflow**, allowing you to dynamically add, view, and organize your daily routines.
- **Dynamic Gen-Z Avatar Stage:** Features a central character whose emoji moods and speech bubble commentary instantly adapt based on your logged habits (e.g., getting roasted for doomscrolling or praised for hitting the gym).
- **Custom Categorization:** Easily log Entertainment, Productivity, or Health activities.
- **Backend Sync:** Export your canvas state directly to a Python Flask backend for daily tracking.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (Vanilla), [Drawflow](https://github.com/jerosoler/Drawflow)
* **Backend:** Python, Flask
* **Production Server:** Gunicorn
* **Hosting:** Render
